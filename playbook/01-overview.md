# Overview

## Purpose

This repository contains my research-backed playbook on **Cold Email Outreach**. It combines insights from experienced practitioners, compares conflicting advice, and presents my own conclusions based on the evidence I collected.

## Who This Playbook Is For

This playbook is intended for:

- Sales Development Representatives (SDRs)
- Recruiters
- Startup founders
- Freelancers
- Agencies
- Anyone who wants to improve cold email response rates

## Goal

The goal is to provide a practical, evidence-based process for writing effective cold emails that generate replies while avoiding common mistakes.

## Research Method

To build this playbook, I:

1. Researched expert LinkedIn posts.
2. Watched YouTube videos from recognized practitioners.
3. Read blogs and articles on cold outreach.
4. Compared different opinions instead of following one expert blindly.
5. Selected recommendations based on reasoning, practical usefulness, and supporting evidence.

## What's Included

- Research-backed recommendations
- Complete step-by-step SOP
- Areas where experts disagree
- Ideas I rejected and why
- My own original ideas
- Limitations of this playbook
- Final conclusions
