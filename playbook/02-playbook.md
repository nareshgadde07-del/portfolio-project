# Cold Email Outreach Playbook (SOP)

## Objective

The objective of this playbook is to create personalized, high-quality cold emails that increase response rates while maintaining professionalism and avoiding spam.

---

# Step 1: Research the Prospect

Before writing an email, spend 2–5 minutes researching the prospect.

Look for:

- Their LinkedIn profile
- Recent posts
- Company news
- Funding announcements
- Hiring activity
- New product launches

**Why?**

A personalized email demonstrates genuine interest and significantly improves the likelihood of receiving a response.

# Step 2: Write a Short Subject Line

Keep the subject line between 3–5 words.

Examples:

- Quick Question
- Idea for Your Team
- About Your Hiring
- Growth Opportunity

Avoid:

- ALL CAPS
- Clickbait
- Excessive punctuation

**Why?**

Simple subject lines are easier to read and generally achieve better open rates.

# Step 3: Personalize the Opening

Do not begin with generic introductions.

Instead, mention something specific.

Examples:

- A recent LinkedIn post
- A company achievement
- A recent funding round
- A new feature launch

**Why?**

People respond better when they know the email was written specifically for them.

# Step 4: Clearly Explain the Value

In one or two sentences explain:

- What you do
- Why it matters
- How it benefits the recipient

Avoid lengthy company introductions.

# Step 5: Keep the Email Short

Target:

80–120 words

Avoid unnecessary background information.

**Why?**

Busy professionals often scan emails quickly.

# Step 6: Use One Clear Call-to-Action

Ask only one question.

Examples:

- Would you be open to a quick conversation next week?
- Is this something your team is currently exploring?

Avoid asking multiple questions.

# Step 7: Follow Up

If there is no reply:

First follow-up: 3 days later

Second follow-up: 7 days later

Final follow-up: 10–14 days later

Remain polite and professional.

# Step 8: Measure Results

Track:

- Open Rate
- Reply Rate
- Positive Replies
- Meetings Booked
